Broom math

"A quick sweep" is never quick, and the broom head dies faster than anyone admits. Broom math turns broom width and stroke into strokes and honest minutes per room, the weekly round into hours a year, and your sweeping pace into how long a broom head really lasts. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Sweep plan - broom width, stroke and room size become strokes and minutes.
  • Week plan - rooms and sweeping pace become minutes a week and hours a year.
  • Head plan - hours a week become months per head, heads and cost per year.

Labeled norms

Labeled stroke: about a second, with a labeled 20% overlap between strokes. Labeled broom-head life: 300 sweeping hours. Labeled broom: 30 cm wide, one-meter stroke. These are reference values, not rules - your broom, your floor and your pace decide the real numbers.
